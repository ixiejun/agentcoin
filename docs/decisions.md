| D38 | Consensus | AC-BFT finality: two-phase voting (prepare, commit) in rounds with a locking rule, threshold `q = ⌊2W/3⌋ + 1`, pipelined rounds, timeouts from two slots backing off ×1.5 up to 30 s, and catch-up from the rounds that members worth more than `W − q` have reached. | D39 | Offences | PoA offence handling: block-seal and AC-BFT vote double signing are reported on chain by unsigned, authorized extrinsics and verified by the runtime. | D40 | Emission | The emission epoch length L is a genesis parameter (3,600 blocks on live chains) and must divide the 126,230,400 blocks of a 4-year halving period; the schedule is `S(e) = (10,500,000 ATC >> n) × L / 126,230,400` (rounded down) with `n = e × L / 126,230,400`, settled at block `(e + 1) × L + 1`. The node enforces the cap without trusting the runtime: minting happens only in settlement blocks, at most `2 × S` per settlement, and cumulative minting never exceeds the cumulative schedule; all burns (80% of fees and tips, reaped dust) are counted in `Emission::TotalBurned`. | D43 | Consensus | PoA → PoS switch (revises D19): one-way. At every validator-epoch boundary the checkpoint passes when all active stake (self-stake plus nominations, excluding unbonding) is at least 10% of the **total issuance**, at least 21 candidates are qualified (active self-stake ≥ 0.1% of the issuance, not paused) and the height is at least 63,115,200 (two years); the conditions must hold at every checkpoint for 604,800 blocks (seven days), any failure restarting the count. The switch replaces all PoA authorities with the elected set; during the seven days every epoch publishes a preview election. Until then the PoA multisig adds and removes PoA authorities (at the next boundary, never emptying the set); afterwards it cannot. The node recomputes every checkpoint from well-known keys and rejects early, delayed or reverted switches; live chains must use these values (constitution layer 1) | m3-pos |
| D44 | Consensus | Nominated PoS: candidates bond at least 0.1% and nominators at least 0.001% of the issuance; each nominator names 1–16 candidates; at most 500 candidates and 750 nominators (the most one block can elect), full lists evict their smallest entry; sequential Phragmén with balancing every validator epoch; the active-set size `K` is a parameter (100 on live chains, at least the switch's candidate count and at most half an epoch, storage bound 1,000, I-004); AC-BFT weight `max(1, backing / 10^12)` | m3-pos |
| D45 | Slashing | Only the validator's own self-stake is slashed, bonded and unbonding alike: vote double signing 100%, seal double signing 10%, the most severe kind per set without adding up; slashed ATC is burned and counted in `Emission::TotalBurned`; nominators are never slashed; going offline costs no principal. | D46 | Rewards | In PoS the security budget is shared between validators by blocks authored, not by stake (R1); each share first pays the validator's commission (5%–100%, increases after 7 days, decreases at the next epoch), the rest goes pro rata to its backing, paid automatically; rounding remainders are never minted. An epoch with a missed randomness reveal earns no points, and three in a row pause the validator from elections; in PoA misses are only counted | m3-pos |

| D54 | Market | Transparent credits (α implementation of D20) are cumulative channel vouchers, not one voucher per request with a nonce. A user escrows ATC with a gateway in a (user, gateway) channel and signs `{genesis hash, user, gateway, channel number, cumulative USD}` with ML-DSA under `agentcoin/voucher/v1`; the chain keeps one redeemed-amount counter per channel, so a replayed or older voucher pays nothing and no nonce set grows. Redemption converts the increment at the reference rate rounding down and pays at most the escrow. Withdrawing escrow and changing the voucher key take effect after a delay (1 day live), during which earlier vouchers still redeem; an emptied channel resets (number + 1) and voids earlier vouchers. | D55 | Market | Market stake is denominated in dollars and converted at the reference rate rounding up: T1 providers $1,000, T2 $100, gateways $1,000 (gateway fee capped at 5%). A provider is serviceable while active, staked at or above its threshold and heartbeating at most two 600-block intervals apart; a heartbeat no earlier than half an interval is free. Provider and gateway stake unbonds over 7 days (604,800 blocks), longer than the settlement challenge period. The slash and jail interface is reserved for M6 audits: no transaction reaches it, not even the PoA administration (D6), and there are no `force_*` calls. | D56 | Market | The ATC/USD reference rate is set by the PoA administration (no oracle in the MVP): positive, at most ±20% from the previous value (bounds inclusive), at least one day (86,400 blocks) after the previous change, every change visible on chain. | D57 | Market | Work settlement follows refine / accumulate. Receipts (`{genesis hash, gateway, provider, job kind, model, request ID, input and output tokens, fee, TOPLOC commitment, TTFT, total time}`, fee at the provider's listed price rounded up to a micro-dollar) are signed with ML-DSA under `agentcoin/receipt/v1` by both provider and gateway and stay off chain. A gateway's work report carries the receipts' BLAKE3 Merkle root, one total per (provider, model) (at most 128, inference only) and at most 16 vouchers; the totals must equal exactly the vouchers' redeemed increments. Of the redeemed ATC, `b` (20%, guardrailed 10–90%) is burned, the gateway's registered fee comes next and the rest is split among providers pro rata to their dollars, rounding down, the remainder burned; fee and shares stay held on the gateway's account. The challenge period (2 emission epochs) is only a delay in M5: no transaction disputes or voids a report; a provider jailed before its work is settled (M6) loses its pending shares (burned) and emission. After maturity anyone claims for an account by (epoch, gateway); reports are kept 720 epochs | m5-work-settlement |
| D58 | Emission | Verified market work of emission epoch `e` is the work (`dollars' ATC × k`, `k` = 50%, guardrailed `k − b ≤ 50%`) of the reports maturing in `e`, and work in its challenge period does not count. Emission mints `min(50% × avail, work)` into the settlement pot, from which each provider claims `market × own work ÷ epoch work` (rounded down; voided work is not paid). The pot keeps one existential deposit forever, taken from the first emission it receives. The node invariants are unchanged | m5-work-settlement |
| D59 | Market | TOPLOC is ported (`ac-toploc`, permissive zone) byte-compatibly from the reference implementation by Prime Intellect at commit `7ab7bcd` (MIT): top-k activations by magnitude, Newton interpolation over the field of 65,497, the reference encoding, comparison results as integers (both reference medians). It is not a cryptographic primitive and has no secrecy or signing role. Vectors are the reference's own output (CPU PyTorch 2.6.0, fixed seeds), regenerated by a script and checked byte for byte in CI. The receipt commits to the proofs with a BLAKE3 hash (`agentcoin 2026-09 toploc-commit v1`); re-checking belongs to M6 audits | m5-work-settlement |
| D60 | Cryptography | Off-chain market traffic (proxy → gateway → provider) runs over a sealed channel at the application layer, independent of TLS. The sender encapsulates with X-Wing to the recipient's key (a provider's is registered on chain; a gateway's is announced at its endpoint, signed with its account's ML-DSA key under `agentcoin/gateway-kem/v1`) and signs the handshake with its account's ML-DSA key under `agentcoin/sealed-channel/v1`. Each direction has its own key derived from the shared secret and the signed handshake (`agentcoin 2026-09 sealed-key v1`) and is sealed with ChaCha20-Poly1305 in chunks of at most 64 KiB, each authenticating its sequence number and a final flag, so reordering, truncation and data after the final chunk are rejected. A handshake is valid for ±120 s and never twice: a bounded replay cache (1,000,000 entries by default) refuses new handshakes when full rather than evict unexpired ones. The wire format has regression vectors; a new format needs a new context version | m5-gateway-provider |
| D61 | Market | Transparent credits (D54) are used postpaid. Each request carries a cumulative voucher equal exactly to the channel's billed total so far; the gateway admits it only if the escrow covers the unredeemed bills plus the maximum fee of every request in flight (the output bound defaults to 4,096 tokens and is written into the forwarded request). After each response the gateway returns the provider- and gateway-signed receipt and the new billed total; the user's side checks the receipt against the chain (signatures, registered keys, listed price) and only then pays exactly the new total. A gateway therefore risks at most the requests in flight, and report totals always equal the redeemed increments. Unmodified OpenAI SDKs reach the market through the wallet's local proxy (`ac-wallet market serve`, loopback only, port 8411 by default), which holds the voucher key, keeps the paid total in a local state file and stops paying at an optional `--max-usd` cap. Anonymous vouchers (D20, D27) remain the mainnet payment path | m5-gateway-provider |
| D62 | Market | Gateway and provider services (`ac-gateway`, `ac-provider`, GPL zone) follow common rules. They never log or store request or output content: logs carry only their own crate's messages (IDs, sizes, timings, error codes), and the only persisted data are receipts, reports and billing state. The gateway routes to the cheapest serviceable provider, breaking ties by the lowest smoothed time to first token, and fails over only before the first chunk; an unreachable provider is suspended for 30 s and one that signs an invalid receipt for 1 hour. Reports are submitted automatically every `--report-interval` blocks (default and maximum: one emission epoch). Gateway and provider keep receipts until the challenge period of their report ends, then delete them. Providers heartbeat at ¾ of the interval, serve only active registered gateways, and claim once per epoch. Receipts carry an all-zero TOPLOC commitment until engine-side proofs arrive | m5-gateway-provider |
| D63 | Market | TOPLOC proofs come from a thin vLLM plugin (`plugins/vllm`, Python, permissive zone; vLLM 0.30, V1 model runner) and are built in Rust. The plugin reads the output of the model's final normalization layer at each forward step and sends, per request and step, only the top-k candidates (indices and bfloat16 bits, larger magnitude first, the lower index among equals) over a local socket (ACTL protocol v1: length-prefixed frames of at most 1 MiB, Hello / Welcome / Segment / Finish) to the provider, which builds the proofs with `ac-toploc`; proofs built from candidates equal those built from the whole activations byte for byte, ties included (the reference implementation leaves ties to `torch.topk`, so it matches only on chunks without a tie at the k-th place). Market parameters: top-k 128, decode batches of 32, prefill not skipped (one prefill chunk and one chunk per 32 decode steps; 258 bytes per proof). The engine starts only with a bfloat16 model, prefix caching and speculative decoding off, no pipeline parallelism and the V1 runner; only tensor-parallel rank 0 sends. The plugin writes no file and logs only counts. Other engines (SGLang), other dtypes, prefix caching and speculative decoding are deferred (I-009..I-011) | m5-engine-toploc |
| D64 | Market | A receipt's TOPLOC commitment binds the proofs, which travel provider → gateway → user: the provider attaches them to the receipt, the gateway checks them (parameters equal the market's, the chunk count matches the output tokens, every proof has the fixed size, the recomputed commitment matches) before co-signing and billing and passes them to the user with the bill; the wallet's local proxy repeats the check and stops paying on a mismatch. An all-zero commitment comes with no proofs and stays valid until M6 (I-008); a non-zero one without matching proofs is an invalid receipt. Provider and gateway store the proofs with the receipt and the report until the challenge period ends and delete them with it; candidates are never stored. Proofs never go on chain; re-checking them against a re-run and the decision thresholds belong to M6 audits (I-007) | m5-engine-toploc |
| D65 | Market | TOPLOC re-check method and judging rules. An auditor (`ac-auditor`, GPL zone) re-checks an inference against its proofs with the same engine and plugin in verify mode (`AGENTCOIN_TOPLOC_MODE=verify`; engine protocol v2 carries the mode, providers accept only prove mode and the auditor only verify mode, v1 is incompatible). It re-creates the prompt and output tokens through the engine's tokenizer, recomputes the whole sequence in one prefill, and the plugin sends the top-k candidates of every row, from which the comparison equals the one from full activations. The outcome is pass, fail or inconclusive. Fail: an all-zero commitment (an audited request without proofs fails, D64 lifted for audits), proofs that do not match the commitment, the parameters or the chunk count, or any chunk outside its bounds (an inference passes only if every chunk passes; the reason names the first failing chunk and its metrics). Inconclusive: the receipt and the case disagree, the tokens cannot be re-created, the re-check engine errs, or the model is not bf16 (only bf16 models are re-checked in the MVP, I-011). The prefill chunk (chunk 0) and the decode chunks have separate bounds; all comparisons are in integers (mean in hundredths). Re-checks log no content | m6-toploc-verify |
| D66 | Market | Re-check thresholds `AUDIT_THRESHOLDS` version 2: prefill chunk exponent mismatches ≤ 2, mean mantissa error ≤ 0.50, median ≤ 1; each decode chunk ≤ 20, ≤ 8.00, ≤ 8. Every judgement carries the threshold version and changed values take a new version. Calibrated with real vLLM 0.30 on CPU (`Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct`, bf16), proving and re-checking with different batching, against four cheats (another model of the same architecture, int8 and int4 weights, a hidden system prompt); CI runs a quick regression on every push. Re-checks on CPUs keep oneDNN off AMX (`ONEDNN_MAX_CPU_ISA=AVX512_CORE_BF16`), and the plugin refuses verify mode on an AMX CPU without it. Acceptance is the M6 target, an honest misjudgment rate below 0.1% per auditor: 1 in 3,000 with no cheat passing (`docs/guides/toploc-calibration.md`). GPU and cross-hardware calibration (I-012) is required before the α testnet | m6-toploc-verify |
| D67 | Market | On-chain audit (`pallet-audit`, ELVES-style random sampling with escalation). Auditors bond stake like providers (live $1,000, guardrailed $100–$100,000, unbond 7 days, at most 1,000; providers and gateways cannot register). Each round of `RoundBlocks` (live 1,800 blocks, shortened to 1,200 by D68) the first block freezes the roster of qualified auditors and draws a seed from on-chain randomness; for each provider `m` = 2 auditors are drawn by a domain-separated BLAKE3 sample (`agentcoin 2026-10 audit-assign v1`), computed on demand, never stored. An assigned auditor submits one verdict per provider within the round: pass, fail with reason or inconclusive, with the threshold version (must equal the accepted version, raised only by the administration) and the double-signed receipt, checked on chain (genesis hash, job kind, fee at the current price, key fingerprints, both ML-DSA signatures, provider, the auditor not a party, request ID not reused). Receipts go on chain; prompts, outputs and proofs never do. A fail carries a 32-byte commitment to SCALE evidence (messages, output, finish reason, usage, receipt, proofs) under `agentcoin 2026-10 audit-evidence v1`. Fails from two different auditors within two rounds open a dispute (one open per provider): N reviewers (live 5, dev 3) are drawn excluding accusers, provider and gateways, quorum Q (live 3, dev 2; 2Q > N) within `VoteBlocks` (live 600); reviewers reject unless the evidence matches its commitment and re-checks as fail. Confirmed: the provider loses 10% of its stake (burned) and is jailed, voiding its unsettled work (D57). Rejected: each accuser loses 10% of its stake (burned) and is forced to exit. Undecided: closed by anyone after the deadline; no one is paid and missed votes are counted. Verdicts and winning reviewers are paid $0.05 (admin-set, $0–$1) from an audit pot (`ac/audit`) funded by transfers or the treasury floor; an empty pot skips payment. Per-provider and per-auditor counters; records kept 7 days and pruned in bounded steps. Genesis parameters are guardrailed and immutable | m6-audit-chain |
| D68 | Market | Mystery-shopper auditor agent (`ac-auditor run`, GPL zone). Each round it reads its assignments and, for each provider, sends one generated prompt (task types × topics × phrasings × random entities, optionally mixed with a prompt bank) at a uniformly random block in the first 75% of the round, through an ordinary transparent-credit channel paid from separate payer accounts (never the auditor account; spending caps, operators fund them from independent sources and rotate them; replaced by anonymous vouchers in M7).
